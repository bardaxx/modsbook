# modsbook

The mods in this book and the words they draw with: what a pane shows a person, not how a mod is built.

## Language

**Spine**:
The vertical column a grilling session is read on, top to bottom, its levels hanging from it in the order they were asked.
_Avoid_: grill tab, horizontal tree

**Level**:
One round of a grilling session as it stands on the spine: the questions that were the frontier at that moment, with the recommendation and what was chosen for each.
_Avoid_: step, row
