# modsbook

The mods in this book and the words they draw with: what a pane shows a person, not how a mod is built.

## Language

**Spine**:
The vertical column a grilling session is read on, top to bottom, its levels hanging from it in the order they were asked.
_Avoid_: grill tab, horizontal tree

**Level**:
One round of a grilling session as it stands on the spine: the questions that were the frontier at that moment, with the recommendation and what was chosen for each.
_Avoid_: step, row

**Trace**:
A line on the spine for something the agent did instead of asking: a fact it looked up, a term it wrote to the glossary, an ADR it recorded.
_Avoid_: side row, event, step

**Wrap-up**:
The point where a spine closes: the summary put to the person once no question is left, and their word on it.
_Avoid_: summary, ending

**Run**:
The stretch of a conversation that works a wayfinder map: it starts when `/wayfinder` is invoked and lasts until the conversation is cleared or a separate grill is asked for. Inside a run, the rounds belong to the map; a map opened by hand to look at is not a run.
_Avoid_: wayfinder mode, session

**Reserve**:
A condition the person attaches to a yes ("yes, but…", "sì, tranne…"). A yes with a reserve does not take the recommendation, and it does not confirm a wrap-up.
_Avoid_: caveat, partial yes

**Charting**:
The map's own spine: what is asked and written while no ticket is claimed, from the first round that names the destination to the rounds held between one ticket and the next.
_Avoid_: destination, map grill
